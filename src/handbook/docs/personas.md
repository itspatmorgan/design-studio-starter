A persona is a short, honest description of a kind of person who uses your product. It exists so that "the user" stops meaning "whoever I'm imagining". Base each one on research where you can, and keep the list short: three to five that you can name from memory.

## How to write one

For each persona, give:

- **A name and role**, plus one line on their situation.
- **Goals**, what they are trying to get done.
- **Frustrations**, what gets in their way today.
- **Context**, when and where they use the product, and how often.
- **What they need from a design**, the thing that decides whether it works for them.

## Example

The persona below is made up, to show the shape. Replace it.

### Sam, on-call analyst

Sam triages the alerts that arrive overnight, often on a small screen and in a hurry.

**Goals:** decide quickly which alerts matter, and hand off cleanly at the end of a shift.

**Frustrations:** too many low-value alerts, and having to open several pages to understand one.

**Context:** a few hours a day, in short bursts, under time pressure.

**Needs from a design:** the decision and its evidence in one place, with no hunting.
