When you first open Home as a registered contributor, a short introduction appears. Each teammate gets their own introduction. You can also explore before registering.

Move through the tour at your own pace, or choose **Skip introduction**. It introduces prototypes and systems and points you toward examples to explore.

Choose **Explore my studio** when ready, or open an example from the last step. The introduction closes, and your next visit opens the studio directly.

The Guide remains available whenever you need it. [Introduction](/documentation/guide) explains the two collections, and [Your first prototype](/documentation/guide/first-prototype) helps you turn an idea into working screens.
