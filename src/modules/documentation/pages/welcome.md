When your studio first opens, a short introduction appears over the home page. Move through it at your own pace, or choose **Skip introduction** to start exploring. It explains the concepts below.

## Bring an idea to life

Describe what you want to explore in your coding agent’s chat. Your agent builds it; you try the result in Design Studio and guide what happens next. Bring your design judgment and product knowledge. Your agent handles the code.

## Keep the whole idea together

A **prototype** holds the screens, the flow, and the thinking behind an idea. Its pieces are called **artifacts**. Depending on your studio’s tools, these include:

- **Views:** working screens to click through and try.
- **Documents:** briefs, notes, and decisions explaining what you’re exploring and why.
- **Diagrams:** flows showing how the experience fits together.
- **Canvases:** an open space to sketch, compare, and arrange artifacts and notes together.

Open an example prototype. Try its screens, then use its navigation to explore the supporting artifacts.

## Build on a system that fits

A **system** gives your prototypes a shared design toolkit and gives your agent guidance about your product. You can use the same system across many prototypes.

- **Theme and components** supply the colors, type, and reusable building blocks that make prototypes feel like your product.
- **Context** describes your product, audience, and design principles so your agent can make decisions that fit.
- **Skills** help your agent carry out tasks in the way your team wants.

Open an example system to see these pieces together.

## Make the studio your own

Your studio is fully customizable. After exploring the examples, work with your agent to adapt them to your product. You can keep and customize an example system, or remove it and bring in your own. You can also adapt an example prototype or start fresh.

Copy the request in the introduction into your coding agent’s chat in the studio folder. It will ask about your idea and audience, help you choose a system, and build a first version you can try. See [Customize your studio](/documentation/guide/customize) for more ideas.

Choose **Explore my studio** when you are ready, or open an example directly from the last step. The introduction closes, and your next visit opens the studio directly. You can use this Guide whenever you want a reminder of the core concepts.
