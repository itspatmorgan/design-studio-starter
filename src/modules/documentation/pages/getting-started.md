If you have Studio running locally, you are ready to explore. You do not need to finish configuring everything before making a prototype.

## Explore the examples

Open **Prototypes** and try an example. Click through its screens, then explore the supporting artifacts in its navigation. Open **Systems** to see the components, styles, and guidance behind it.

The starter includes Product and Marketing systems, plus Feedback Inbox and Design Studio Marketing prototypes. They are examples you can use, adapt, or replace. An existing team's studio may have different content.

Keep examples while they are useful. Ask your agent to help remove unused examples later, checking which prototypes depend on their systems.

## Connect your agent to this studio

Continue in the studio folder your agent opened. Seeing a preview does not necessarily mean your coding app has that folder selected. If you are unsure, ask:

> Confirm that we are working in the folder for this running Design Studio. Show me where my work will be saved.

If you are joining a team, ask your agent to register you as a contributor in its existing studio. This gives you your own place for prototypes while preserving the team's systems and setup.

## Choose your next step

To try an idea now, follow [Your first prototype](/documentation/guide/first-prototype). You can use an existing system while learning.

If using your product's components and styles matters from the start, follow [Systems](/documentation/guide/systems#bring-your-own-system). Bring any component source, brand materials, or product context you already have.

A new studio starts in Personal mode. When you want team use, ask your agent to help configure it or change the setting locally. [Customize your studio](/documentation/guide/customize#configure-the-studio) explains the options.

## Return later or create another studio

Open this same folder in your coding app and ask “Open my Design Studio.” Your saved work stays in that folder.

For installation, another studio, or a teammate's first setup, use the [public setup instructions](https://github.com/itspatmorgan/design-studio-starter/blob/main/SETUP.md). They cover agent-assisted setup through plugins or directly from source. Personal local use does not require GitHub or hosting.
